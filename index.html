BESI Sorter Attendance Portal

A phone-first time clock for warehouse sorters, with a management console behind it. I designed, built and run it.

The problemAttendance was consolidated by hand every cutoff. Forgotten Time Outs and shift mix-ups led to corrections and disputes.

What it does nowSorters clock in from their phones inside the warehouse. Hours, late, undertime and overtime come from the shift they actually worked, and cutoff reports export to Excel with remarks.
